Lucent Secret Santa

Draw the family Secret Santa over WhatsApp. Everyone gets their own link and sees only who they drew.

This page is not open at a web address (it was opened as a file or in a preview, for example), so it can’t send links from here. You can do the draw with everyone together: each person taps their own name and sees who they drew on this device, one at a time. To send links over WhatsApp, open the published address, lucentcrypto.org/en/amigo-oculto/.

2 · Who takes part

example: replace with your family’s names
  • Phone or e-mail (optional): to send the link straight to the person, by WhatsApp or e-mail. Stays on this device only. Write phone numbers with the country code, e.g. +1 555 010 0000.
  • Helper: the number of the person who opens the link together with them (for example, 3 for Ann to help the grandparents). A helped person never draws their own helper.
  • Columns 1, 2, 3…: mark who belongs to the same couple or household. People marked in the same column never draw each other. A person can be in more than one column.

Swipe the table sideways to see all columns and the remove button.

3 · Draw

The draw happens on this phone or computer. Nobody, not even you, sees the pairs on screen.

4 · Everyone sees who they drew

How will each person see it?

Updating changes only what is needed: whoever joined gets a match, and one or two people get a new match, marked below. Everyone else stays the same. Note: when you send the new links, you will be able to work out who drew whom in that swap.

Redo the draw

Links already sent keep opening the old draw, shown with its date and time. Tell the group to use only the new links.

5 · Keep a recovery link

If you lose this phone or clear the browser data, this link brings the draw back, with the same links. It contains the whole draw, so keep it to yourself: send it to yourself by e-mail or in your WhatsApp chat with yourself.
