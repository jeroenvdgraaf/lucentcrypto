Lucent Encrypt

A 7-word phrase becomes your key. You publish a link, and anyone can use it to encrypt a message that only you can open, with the phrase. Nothing is stored anywhere: everything happens on this page. Three modes, side by side: classic (X25519), post-quantum (ML-KEM-768) and the hybrid of the two (X-Wing).

Don’t use it for anything of value

Don’t use it for bank passwords, cryptocurrency wallet keys or anything of value. It is a demo, made with care, but without guarantees and without an independent audit. Lose the phrase and you lose the messages, forever.

1. Your identifier

The identifier is visible in every link. Use a name if you don’t want to expose a phone number or e-mail. To recreate the key later, you will need the phrase and this identifier, typed the same way.

2. Your phrase

Want to keep the phrase more safely? Split it among people you trust with Lucent Secret. The words come from the Portuguese BIP-39 list, the same on both language versions, so a phrase works on either.

What this demo guarantees, and what it does not
  • Only the phrase opens it. Nobody else reads the message, not even LucentCrypto, which never sees anything: there is no server.
  • Quantum computers: in classic mode, a future quantum computer could read today’s messages. Post-quantum mode is designed to resist. The hybrid stays secure as long as either of the two holds.
  • The phrase is everything. Anyone with the public link can try to guess the phrase, forever and with unlimited attempts. That is why the page draws the phrase at random (77 bits) and each attempt costs a slow derivation (Argon2id). A phrase you made up yourself would not be secure.
  • Unknown sender. The recipient doesn’t know who encrypted. There are no signatures.
  • Links can be forged. Someone can publish a link in another person’s name. The defense is the fingerprint, checked another way.
  • Does not protect: against malware on the device or someone watching the phrase being typed; files are not supported (text only, up to 2000 characters).
