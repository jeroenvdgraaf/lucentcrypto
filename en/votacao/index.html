Lucent Vote

Secret votes during a meeting, in person or online, with up to 20 people (for more, contact us). The same participants vote on several questions, one at a time, as the organizer opens them, including any that come up on the spot. Everyone votes on their phone, with a personal link that is valid for the whole meeting. The encrypted ballots are published on a public page that anyone can check. They are added up while still encrypted, and only the total of each vote is decrypted: no ballot is ever opened.

    What this demo guarantees, and what it does not
    • Secret ballot: each ballot is encrypted on the voter’s phone. Only the totals of each vote are opened. With the split key, neither the organizer nor LucentCrypto alone can read a ballot.
    • Counted as cast: everyone sees their own ballot on the board, and anyone can check that the result is exactly the sum of the accepted ballots. The board has a hash chain: its history cannot be rewritten without the pages noticing.
    • Who voted is shown (by name or by a code, with the time); how they voted is not.
    • The organizer controls the list. Every addition and removal appears on the board. With names shown, everyone can spot an unexpected name.
    • Sign-up by group link: whoever signs up through the group link creates their own key on their phone, and the organizer cannot vote in that person’s place. For people the organizer adds by hand, they could: the board shows how each person joined.
    • Small groups: when there are few participants, or when everyone votes the same way, the result can reveal how each person voted.
    • Does not protect against: someone voting while another person watches, or passing on their link; malware on the phone; losing the organizer link.
    • Everything is deleted 24 hours after the first midnight (Brasília time) following the end of the meeting. A downloaded record can still be checked.
