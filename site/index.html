How it works

01

Seal

The rules and the data are fixed beforehand, with a timestamp. Nobody can change anything afterwards without it showing.

02

Public proof

The result comes with a mathematical proof. Personal data stays protected.

03

Verify

Anyone can check it in the browser, or with an independent, open-source verifier.
